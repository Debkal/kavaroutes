    Automatic invoice reminders & payment receipts
    The editable Payment confirmation rule queues a receipt when you record a confirmed payment. It includes the paid invoice, amount, date and reference. Enable emails for that customer first. This ledger does not independently verify bank settlement. No receipts are backfilled for payments recorded before a rule or customer email was enabled.
    Each due-date rule sends once per issued invoice on the chosen day, at or after the UTC hour. Use -3 for three days before, 0 for due date, or 7 for seven days overdue. Rules apply to all email-enabled, nonsuspended customers. Paid/voided invoices are skipped. No catch-up for dates missed while delivery was paused. Existing queued messages keep their content; pause a rule to cancel its waiting messages.

      The API read default approximates current polling (60 requests/user/minute). Try 2 to explore a future event-driven design; that optimization is not yet deployed. Monthly active users are a conservative proxy for simultaneous users, not a concurrency measurement.
      
        
      Edit scenario assumptions
      
        
      Assumes every listed driver is transmitting and every office user is active, 12 application requests per trip, and one monthly payment per business. Maps estimates include tiered Essentials routes/geocoding and Dynamic Maps with shared free caps; exclude route-matrix elements, fleet optimization, SMS and special SKUs. Add those to variable costs. Payment assumption: 2.9% + $0.30 processing plus 0.7% Billing. Tax, refunds, acquisition cost and owner compensation must be budgeted separately or included in overhead.
      
        
      Measured September 22, 2026: 25 read requests/sec for 180 seconds, zero errors, p95 78 ms. At a 50/sec target, throughput reached 44.6/sec with p95 821 ms and 114 unscheduled requests. This small synthetic-tenant test excludes writes, GPS ingestion and Cloudflare; the load generator shared the VM.
      
        
      API traffic and socket counts are workload estimates, not a capacity guarantee. Current VM: e2-medium, 4 GB RAM; realtime is capped at 50 total sockets. A 100-business production rollout still needs tenant isolation, sustained mixed read/write and GPS tests, backup/restore, and availability validation.
